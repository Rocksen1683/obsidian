## Feedback 
![[Pasted image 20241103170338.png]]

The [[System Stability]] of the *feedback loop* will be based on whether $F(s)$ which is the transfer function of the loop is stable. 

We can check this by calculating $F(s)$ which is done by: 
$$F(s) = \frac{G(s)}{1 + G(s)}$$
