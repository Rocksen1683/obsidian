Given a characteristic equation 
$$F(s) = a_{0}s^{n} + a_{1}s^{n-1} + .... + a_{n} = 0$$
## Method of forming Routh's Array
![[Pasted image 20241202131121.png]]
The first row of the [[Routh Array]] will be all the *even* coefficients of the characteristic polynomial and the second row will be all the *odd* coefficients. 

After these two rows, we can calculate the remaining using the formula method in the image.
