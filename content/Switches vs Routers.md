Both [[Ethernet Switch]] and *routers* are *store-and-forward* objects and both have *forwarding tables*. 

**Router**:
- Are [[Network Layer]] devices and examine [[Network Layer]] headers 
- Computes tables using routing algorithms and IP Addresses 

**Switch**:
- Are [[Link Layer]] devices and examine [[Link Layer]] headers
- Learn forwarding tables using *flooding*, *self-learning*, and [[MAC Address]]
