Is basically [[CSMA]] with *collision detection*. With this we can detect collisions within a *short time* and can also abort colliding transmissions which implies a reduce in channel wastage. 

NOTE: *collision detection* is easy in wired but is difficult with wireless. 

With [[CSMA-CD]], we reduce the amount of time wasted in collisions and also abort transmission on collision detection.
