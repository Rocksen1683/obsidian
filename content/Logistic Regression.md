A model that provides prediction with confidence. 

## [[Bernoulli Model]]

Based on the [[Bernoulli Model]], our logistic regression will work in the following:
- If $P(X, w)$ is greater than 1/2, then we predict $\hat{y}$ as $1$
- Else $0$

With this, we wouldn't need the data to be [[Linear Seperability]]. We also don't only get predictions, but also get the *confidence*  of the prediction due to the [[Sigmoid]] of $<X, w>$.
