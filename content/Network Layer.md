The *network layer* is responsible for transporting *segments* from senders to receivers. 

The senders encapsulate *segments* into *datagrams* and pass it to the [[Link Layer]]. The receivers deliver segments to the [[Transport Layer]]. 

 [[Routers]] -> part of the [[Network Layer]].
