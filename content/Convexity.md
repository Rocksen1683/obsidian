A *convex function* is one that looks like a U on the graph. 

A function is convex iff for all $\lambda \in [0, 1], x_{1}, x_{2}$, 
$$f(\lambda x_{1} + (1- \lambda )x_{2}) \leq \lambda f(x_{1}) + (1 - \lambda)f(x_{2})$$


Alternatively, the [[Hessian]] of f(x) which is $\nabla ^{2}f(x)$ is [[Positive Semidefinite]] which means 

$$\nabla ^{2}f(x) \succeq 0$$

Having a *convex* function is good as it makes optimization easier.
