Let $A$ be the matrix with rows $a_{i}$. 

$$ A = \begin{bmatrix} a_{1} \\ \\
a_{2} \\
... \\  \\
a_{n}
\end{bmatrix}$$

$$Az = \begin{bmatrix} <a_{1}, z > \\ \\
< a_{2}, z> \\
... \\  \\
<a_{n}, z>
\end{bmatrix}$$
The goal now would be to not change when $Az > \vec{0}$
