Before using the [[Routh-Hurwitz Criterion]], ensure that you verify the following criterions of stability for the characteristic polynomial:
1. Ensure that all *coefficients* of the char polynomial have the same sign
2. None of the *coefficients* of the char polynomial vanishes, i.e, all of the powers of the polynomial are present

If a polynomial satisfies the above condition, then we can say it is a [[Hurwitz Polynomial]] and can be used for the [[Routh-Hurwitz Criterion]].
