*Carrier Sense Multiple Access* can overcome the inefficiencies of [[Slotted ALOHA]]. 

In simple CSMA, you *listen* before you transmit and:
- if the channel was sensed as *idle*: transmit the entire frame 
- if the channel was sensed as *busy*: defer the transmission 

## Collisions 

*Collisions* can still occur with carrier sensing: 
- propagation delay means two nodes may not hear each other's just-started transmission 

In the case of a *collision*, entire packet transmission time is wasted and the distance and propagation delay.
